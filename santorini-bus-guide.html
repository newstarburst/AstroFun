Santorini Bus

—:—
Need to know
Cash only. A conductor comes down the aisle and takes euros. No cards, no Apple Pay. Keep small notes and coins.
Buy a ticket on each bus. If you change buses, you pay twice. There are no day passes or transfers.
Look for the town name, not a number. The destination is on a card in the windshield.
Kids. Under 7 free, ages 7–12 half price. Luggage is free and goes underneath.
Stuck? Fira taxi rank: +30 22860 22555. Bus station: +30 22860 25404. Emergency: 112.
Times are 24-hour. 22:50 means 10:50 p.m.

Official KTEL timetable, revised 14 July 2026.
Schedules shift in season — check the printed board at Fira station.
